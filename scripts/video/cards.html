Read-Along Coach
A reading coach that listens patiently.
Helps only when a child is truly stuck. Never marks them wrong when it couldn't hear clearly.
Built on AssemblyAI Universal-Streaming
The problem
One teacher.
Thirty readers.

Reading is learned aloud

Someone has to wait while a child sounds out a word — and help at the right moment.

Attention is scarce

One-to-one listening is the thing a busy classroom has least of.

Fluency is hand-timed

Words correct per minute, measured with a stopwatch, one child at a time.

The fairness rule
Unclear is never wrong.
The cat did not move.

AssemblyAI heard “moo” with low confidence.

Confidence below threshold → “couldn't hear”

Grey, italic, dotted. Never red.

Left out of every score

Not in accuracy, not in words-per-minute, not in the practice list.

How it works
AssemblyAI understands speech.
We decide when to help.
